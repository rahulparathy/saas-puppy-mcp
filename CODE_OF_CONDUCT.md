# Code of Conduct

This is a small project. We want it to stay friendly and useful.

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Stay on topic in issues and pull requests.
- Assume good intent, and say so when you are unsure.
- Help newcomers. Not everyone here is a developer.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, insults, discrimination, personal attacks, sharing someone's private information, and spam.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and block people who break these rules. To report a problem, use GitHub's "Report content" on the comment, or open a private security advisory under the Security tab.
